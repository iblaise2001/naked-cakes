# Naked Cakes

## Description (150 words)

Naked Cakes is an American-inspired dessert and comfort food café near Brussels, run by Jessie: loud laugh, big glasses, soft heart, zero chill. Her old site hid the menu in two images and sent every cake order to an email app. We rebuilt it around her voice and materials. The headline is the product: "Good cakes, good cookies, good vibes" set as three layers of red velvet sponge with piped cream cheese frosting and a cherry on top, because every Naked Cakes cake has two layers of joy. The hot-pink graph paper, diner checkerboard, ribbon banners and the logo's hard navy shadow come straight from her cake guide and packaging. The menu is now real text in three languages. A three-step cake builder with a live price replaces the mailto link. It loads in under two seconds on slow phones, with 3 KB of JavaScript, and scores 100 for accessibility.

## Technologies

Astro 5 (static), TypeScript, hand-written CSS with design tokens, cross-document View Transitions, Speculation Rules prerendering, CSS scroll-driven animations, Cloudflare Pages and Pages Functions, AVIF/WebP image pipeline (sharp), self-hosted variable fonts (Anton, Bricolage Grotesque, Caveat), potrace logo vectorisation, Playwright and axe-core, Lighthouse CI.
